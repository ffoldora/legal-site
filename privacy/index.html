Foldora Privacy Policy

Foldora is a local origami record app for iPhone. This policy explains what stays on your device and what leaves it.

Data on this device

Outlines, fold runs, step notes, photos, and voice recordings are stored in the app sandbox on this device. Foldora does not require an account.

A photo you take or choose is kept with the step note you attach it to. A voice recording is kept as audio on this device. If you allow speech recognition and choose to transcribe a voice note, Apple may process that audio on its servers to produce text.

Purchases

Optional fold packs are sold by Apple. Foldora receives the purchase result so it can add folds on this device. Payment details stay with Apple.

Sharing

Foldora does not upload your photos, audio, notes, or device records to a Foldora server. An archive is created only when you ask for it, and you choose where that file goes.

If you load the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use, the app requests that page from the hosted site. That request includes ordinary connection data handled by the host.

Foldora does not sell personal information or send your photos and notes to a third-party service for processing. Optional speech transcription may send the selected voice recording to Apple after you grant speech recognition permission. You can withhold permission for the camera, photo library, microphone, or speech recognition and still type notes.
