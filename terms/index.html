Foldora Terms of Use

These terms cover the Foldora iPhone app from the publisher that uses the contact address below. By using Foldora you agree to these terms.

What Foldora does

Foldora keeps origami outlines, sheet sizes, fold runs, and step notes on your device. You can take a photo, choose a photo, or record a voice note for a step.

Folds

The app includes a small starting balance of folds. Saving a run as active uses one fold. Creating an archive uses one fold. Draft notes do not use a fold. Additional folds are optional consumable purchases processed by Apple. Spent folds are not returned by a separate restore action.

Your records

You are responsible for the notes and photos you store. Erasing local data removes outlines you created, runs, photos, and voice notes from this device. Purchased folds stay. Uninstalling the app can remove local records if they are not in a device backup you control.

Acceptable use

Do not use Foldora to store content you do not have permission to keep. Outlines you write are yours to record. Shelf outlines shipped with the app are for personal folding practice.
